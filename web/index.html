            About these figures

            Reference price. DMD is not on an exchange yet, so it has no market price. The reference price is the rate the DebitMyData Gateway uses for DMD payments: the higher of DebitMyData's minimum rate and the mining cost, as the gateway records it every 5 minutes (its API). It is not a price anyone can sell DMD at.

            Mining cost. The electricity it takes to mine 1 DMD: the hashes a block takes (averaged over the last week of blocks), at 20 joules per terahash and $0.06 per kWh, divided by the DMD the block creates. Before the gateway existed (October 2026), it is computed from the chain with the same figures.

            Hashrate is worked out from the blocks: the work they took divided by the time they took. Difficulty is the chain's own, adjusted at every block so that one comes every 2.5 minutes on average.

            Market cap is the reference price × the DMD mined so far. Trading volume is what the exchanges trade in 24 hours, from CoinGecko, once DMD is listed on an exchange. On-chain volume is the DMD the chain's transactions sent to other addresses: change sent back to the sender, and the mining rewards, are left out.
